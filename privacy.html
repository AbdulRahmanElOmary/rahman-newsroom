Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Data Processing

Rahman Newsroom processes only the following data: your own TikTok authorization credentials (access and refresh tokens), stored in a private file on your own computer and never transmitted to anyone else; the video files you create and upload to your own TikTok inbox; and basic account information returned by TikTok (such as display name and public video statistics) if requested by the tool during normal operation.

No Third-Party Data Collection

No data from other TikTok users is collected, sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes. This tool operates exclusively with your own account data.

No Tracking or Analytics

No cookies are used. This site contains no analytics tracking. GitHub may maintain its own server logs if the site is hosted there.

Revoking Access

You can revoke the tool's access to your TikTok account at any time by visiting your TikTok app's settings: Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and websites. You can also delete the stored token file manually from your computer.
