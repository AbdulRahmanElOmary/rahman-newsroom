Rahman Newsroom

Rahman Newsroom is a personal tool of an individual developer. It prepares short news videos and uploads them as drafts to the developer's own TikTok inbox through TikTok's Content Posting API (upload to inbox). The developer reviews every draft in the TikTok app and publishes it by hand. The tool does not post automatically, does not collect data from other TikTok users, and is not offered to the public.
